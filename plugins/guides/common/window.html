The window

{NAME} opens as a window of its own, centred on the QGIS screen the first time. Use the system's title bar to move it, maximise, minimise or close it; it does not dock into the QGIS window. {NAME} remembers its place and size, or that it was maximised, and opens the same way next time, also after QGIS restarts. Reset window in the project menu brings it back to its default size, centred.
