Moving through the steps

Under the step pages a bar always says where you are and what comes next:

  • ‹ Back goes to the previous step.
  • The sentence in the middle says whether the step is done (✓, green), needs a check (⚠, amber: you can continue), is not done yet (○, with the button to press), or is optional.
  • Next: <step> › goes to the next step. It is greyed out until a required step is done; on optional steps it reads Skip to <step> ›.
  • On the last step it becomes Continue in AlignPro › (from PosPro) or Continue in CloudPro › (from AlignPro). It opens the next plugin on the same project once this one has published its results; until then it says what is missing.

The step list on the left still opens any step at any time; the bar only guides. CloudPro, an editor rather than a list of pages, shows its three steps (Georeference, Clean & classify, Publish) in a strip under the header with one Next button that does the next thing.

Floating or docked

{NAME} opens as a floating window the first time, centred on the QGIS screen, with its own minimise, maximise and close buttons. It docks into the QGIS window only when you choose to:

  • Dock right, left, bottom or top puts it on that side of the QGIS window. Dragging the window never docks it, so it cannot snap into a corner by accident.
  • Floating window takes a docked window out again; double-clicking the title bar of a docked window does the same.
  • Maximise fills the screen; choose it again (it reads Restore window size) to go back.
  • Reset window returns to the default floating window, centred, at its default size.

{NAME} remembers how you left it (floating with its place and size, maximised, or docked with its width or height) and opens the same way next time, also after QGIS restarts.
