about=StripPro corrects PosPro's trajectory with the Lidar itself. Where the INS recording has holes (mapper 2.9.0 captures lost about a third of the Certus data), the trajectory is a straight line across each hole and the points there are tilted and shifted; the Lidar kept recording, and a surface it sees before, during and after a hole carries the motion through it. Overlapping lines are aligned at the same time. The correction is per line and changes over time: pinned where the trajectory had data, free inside holes, smooth everywhere. Align strips, look at the before and after, Finalize: CloudPro then georeferences with the corrected trajectory. Needs the DCS Core plugin. Menu: Plugins > Canadian Lidar > Data Systems.
