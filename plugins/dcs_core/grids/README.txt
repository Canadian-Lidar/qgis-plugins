ca_nrc_NAD83v70VG.tif
  NAD83(CSRS) velocity grid NAD83v70VG (version 7.0.0) from Natural Resources Canada, Canadian Geodetic
  Survey, in the GeoTIFF form distributed by the PROJ project (PROJ-data, ca_nrc/).
  Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Canada
  (https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
  Used by dcs_core.epochs to move NAD83(CSRS) coordinates between reference epochs (e.g. the observation
  date and New Brunswick's 1997.0).
