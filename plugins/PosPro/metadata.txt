version=1.6.3
author=Canadian Lidar
email=seantobrien@gmail.com
about=PosPro processes Advanced Navigation Certus logs (.anpp) and mapper captures (.clraw) directly. It extracts the raw IMU and raw GNSS observations, downloads ephemeris, precise products and base-station data, computes base positions by PPP or CSRS-PPP, and runs a tightly or loosely coupled GNSS/INS filter forward and backward with a combined (smoothed) result. Quality is shown on the map and in plots, and the trajectory is exported as SBET/SMRMSG or text. A calibration step solves the antenna lever arms from the drive. Styled like the Mobile Mapper dashboard. Processing runs in a separate engine process (RTKLIB-EX inside), so a fault never closes QGIS.
