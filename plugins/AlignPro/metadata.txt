about=AlignPro calibrates the Lidar mounting angles of the mobile mapper from its .clraw captures. It opens the job's shared processing project, uses the trajectory PosPro published, pairs the passes, finds planar surfaces seen by several passes and solves roll, pitch and heading with a point-to-plane adjustment; leaving each line out in turn gives the SDEV. A Data View shows plan, profile and section of the points live while roll, pitch, heading, lever arm and latency are adjusted by hand. The applied values are published for CloudPro (alignment.json) and given in the mapper's mounting convention, ready for Settings > Hardware > Survey sensor. Quality control, sweeps, PDF and text reports and calibration history. Needs the DCS Core plugin. Menu: Plugins > Canadian Lidar > Data Systems.
